# Sound sources

Both sounds are CC0 (public domain) recordings from Freesound.org, trimmed and re-encoded
locally to keep each file under 20 KB.

## shutter.mp3

- Source: "Camera shutter click" by JoMungus
- URL: https://freesound.org/people/JoMungus/sounds/718021/
- Licence: CC0 (page states: "You can copy, modify, distribute and perform the sound,
  even for commercial purposes, all without the need of asking permission.")
- Original: the sound of a Canon EOS 30D taking a picture, ~1s MP3 preview.
- Edit: short fade-out added, re-encoded to 64 kbps MP3 (~8.5 KB).

## advance.mp3

- Source: "35mm film camera winding + SUPER SLOW shutter click" by keverlyhills
- URL: https://freesound.org/people/keverlyhills/sounds/585476/
- Licence: CC0 (page states: "You can copy, modify, distribute and perform the sound,
  even for commercial purposes, all without the need of asking permission.")
- Original: the lever-winding and shutter-firing sound of a Pentax Spotmatic SP 35mm
  camera; a ~2s clip covering the ratcheting wind-lever action (before the separate,
  much longer "super slow" shutter section later in the recording) was trimmed out.
- Edit: trimmed to the wind-lever ratchet, short fades in/out added, re-encoded to
  64 kbps MP3 (~16 KB).
