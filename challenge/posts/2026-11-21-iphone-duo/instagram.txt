Fonder opens up on iPhone Duo.

Unfold the phone and the editor opens up with it. Your photo takes the left side, and the Lab takes the right, so the film, the tone and the colour are all in view while you work. Nothing covers the picture.

Swipe for the Darkroom: thirteen settings on one screen, no scrolling. Then the editor, Curves and Lights.

Fold it halfway through an edit and you carry on where you were.

Coming in the next build, starting with the editor and the Lab. The camera comes after.
