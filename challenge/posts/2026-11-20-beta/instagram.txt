Fonder is opening to its first 100 testers.

It's a film camera for iPhone. You choose a film recipe, shoot, and the photo comes out finished. Load a roll and you get 12, 24 or 36 frames with no preview until you develop it. Every original is kept for 30 days, so you can change your mind and develop a photo again with another recipe.

Nothing is collected and no photo leaves your phone.

I'm building it alone, and I'd love your eyes on it before it reaches the App Store. Join on TestFlight through the link in my bio, shoot for a few days, and tell me what feels off.
