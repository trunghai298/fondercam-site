New recipe: Flamingo 200, inspired by the GTA VI trailers.

That hour when the sky over the sea goes hot pink and orange, the palms turn to silhouettes and a neon sign comes on. I wanted the camera to see it that way, so I built a recipe for it on Fonder's Dusk film.

It started from a Lightroom edit. At full strength it turned every face orange, so I pulled the oranges and reds back until skin stayed warm and the sky kept its pink. Shadows go blue, and lights get a soft glow.

Swipe through a blue-hour street and a beach at sunset. The last frame is the first one before the recipe.

It's free, and it lands in the next build.
