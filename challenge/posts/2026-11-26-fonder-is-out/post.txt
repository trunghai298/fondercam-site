Fonder is out. Today, on the App Store.

A film camera for iPhone, made in Hanoi. Pick one of 66 recipes and see the look in the viewfinder before you shoot. Load a roll of 24, shoot it blind, then watch the whole roll develop at once. Shoot RAW and change the recipe for thirty days.

51 recipes are free. One purchase unlocks the rest and every recipe added later. No subscription.

Go shoot something.
