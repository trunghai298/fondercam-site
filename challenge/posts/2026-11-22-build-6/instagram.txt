Fonder build 6 is out for testers.

The big one is Blended double exposure. Take a photo of one clear subject, then a second of a scene to fill it. Fonder finds the subject and blends the two inside its outline, on white. A person filled with a street. A can filled with a sky.

Swipe for the rest:

65:24, the wide panoramic frame, for when the scene is longer than it is tall. Thank you @hiroshiandhiscamera for recommending it.

On an open iPhone Duo, the editor and the Lab now sit side by side, with the photo on the left and every setting on the right.

The zoom ring now lands on the right lens when you zoom out and back in.

Five recipes have new sample photos: Lantern Street 800, Back Lot 800, Matinee 250, Garden Party 160 and Postcard 100.

If you're testing, try a double exposure first and tell me how long it takes and whether it found your subject.
