Fonder build 6 is out for testers.

The big one is Blended double exposure. Take a photo of one clear subject, then a second of a scene, and Fonder blends the two inside the subject's outline on white.

Also new: a 65:24 panoramic frame, the editor and Lab side by side on an open iPhone Duo, and a zoom ring that lands on the right lens every time.

Thank you @hiroshiandhiscamera for the 65:24 idea. It was the right call.
