Privacy Policy

Effective 29 September 2026

In short

  • Your photos stay on your iPhone. Fonder has no servers that receive them.
  • The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Fonder never asks for your location.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.
  • This website counts visits with Google Analytics only if you agree (see The website).

Who we are

Fonder is an iPhone camera app made by an independent developer, Trung Hai (“we”). This policy explains what the app does with your information. If you have a question, write to haint180298@gmail.com.

Information Fonder does not collect

We don't collect, store on a server, sell or share personal data. Fonder has no user accounts and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software, and it doesn't use your data to track you across other apps or websites.

Photos and videos

  • Where they're kept: photos and videos you take or import are kept on your device, inside Fonder's own storage.
  • Saving to your library: Fonder adds your finished photos and videos to your Photos library when you choose to save them, or automatically if Save to Photos is on in Settings. You can turn this off at any time.
  • Originals: original captures (such as RAW files) are kept for a limited time or storage size so you can re-edit, then removed automatically. Favourites and film-roll photos waiting to be developed are kept.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored inside it. Photos already saved to your Photos library stay there.

Permissions the app asks for

Camera

To take photos and videos. The picture is processed on your device.

Microphone

Only to record sound with videos in Cine mode. Sound is written into the video file on your device.

Photos

To save your finished photos and videos, and to show your recent photos on the Import screen so you can pick one to edit. Fonder only opens the photo you choose.

You can change any of these in the iPhone's Settings app, under Fonder.

Location

Fonder doesn't request or use your location. A photo you import may already contain location information recorded by another camera or app. Fonder keeps that information with the photo, as it was, when you save or share the photo as it is. The Card and Overlay share styles remove location information before sharing.

Sharing

When you share a photo or video, Fonder creates the file on your device and hands it to the destination you choose through the iPhone's share sheet. What happens next is governed by that app or service's privacy policy. A recipe is shared as a QR code that Fonder draws on your screen for another phone to scan. A shared recipe contains only its settings, and a name only if you add one.

New recipes

To show recipes released after you installed the app, Fonder downloads a small list of recipes from a public web address (trunghai298.github.io, hosted by GitHub). The request carries no account, identifier, cookies or personal data. Like any website, the host can see the network address of the device making the request; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Purchases

Fonder's paid unlock (“Unlock everything”) is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether the purchase is active on your device.

The website

Everything above is about the Fonder app, which collects nothing. This section is only about this website, fondercam.online.

  • Visit counts, only if you agree: the site uses Google Analytics to count visits, and only after you choose OK on the banner at the bottom of the page. Until you do — and if you choose No thanks — the site doesn't load Google Analytics or send anything to Google.
  • What Google receives once you agree: your device's network (IP) address, which pages you visit and when, how you arrived, the parts of the homepage you scroll to and the links you use there, and basic information about your browser and device, such as its type, language and screen size. We ask Google to shorten the IP address.
  • Your choice is kept in your browser, on this device only. To change it, clear this site's data in your browser and the banner will ask again.
  • Google handles this information under Google's privacy policy.

Children

Fonder isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we don't hold personal data about you, there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. Everything Fonder keeps is on your device, and you can remove it by deleting photos in the app or deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling information differently, we'll update this page and change the effective date above before that version of the app is released.
