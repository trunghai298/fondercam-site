# fondercam.online homepage — design

Date: 2026-09-27 · Status: draft for review

## 1. Intent

**What Hai asked for:** a homepage that isn't a plain landing page of text and screenshots. It should have animated elements and transitions that combine a vintage and retro feel.

**Agreed in the brainstorm:**
- **Job (option 4):** sell the look now. One action slot moves through three stages: *follow for the beta* now, then *TestFlight*, then the *App Store*.
- **World (option 4):** the story of one roll, told through three period worlds. Loading and shooting are an 80s–90s point-and-shoot; developing is a darkroom; the prints are a lab counter.
- **Language (option 2):** English and Vietnamese, with an EN / VI switch.
- **Build (approach A):** static HTML, CSS and JS with GSAP + ScrollTrigger, no build framework. EN and VI pages are generated by a small Python script.
- **Action now (option 1):** follow for the beta, on Instagram <https://www.instagram.com/fonder.app> and Threads <https://www.threads.com/@fonder.app>. No emails are collected.

**Audience:** mostly people arriving from Hai's social posts, on phones, often on mobile data.

**Success means:**
1. A visitor understands what Fonder does (films, recipes, Film Roll) without reading paragraphs.
2. The motion feels like film and cameras, not a generic scroll-jacked site.
3. The first screen shows in about 1 s on 4G.
4. It scrolls smoothly on an older iPhone.
5. Lighthouse performance and accessibility are both ≥ 90.
6. The page works with Reduce Motion on and with JavaScript off.
7. Switching the action to TestFlight or the App Store is a one-line config change.

**Assumptions** (not stated by Hai, and open to correction):
- The page is always the same visual world, whatever the phone's light or dark setting.
- There's no sound unless the visitor turns it on.
- Vietnamese copy is a draft for Hai to check.

## 2. The story (chapters)

Scroll drives the story. Each chapter pins while its one signature moment plays, over about 1–1.5 screens of scroll, then releases. The whole page is about 7–8 screens.

| # | Chapter | World | Signature moment | Content |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Load a roll | Camera bag at night | A real Fonder canister rolls in and stops; the film leader pulls out to the right as scrolling starts | "Fonder: a film camera for iPhone", plus the language switch |
| 1 | Shoot | 90s compact camera | Hai's frames advance through a viewfinder, one click each, with an orange seven-segment date stamp ticking in the corner; one flash pop reveals a frame in its film look | The film count (one `filmCount` value in `config.json`, set to the app's count when a film ships); recipes you set like a camera; Still, Cine and Roll modes |
| 2 | Develop | Darkroom, red safelight | An iPhone shows Fonder's Developing screen as live HTML: as you scroll the percentage counts up, the stage bar runs Develop → Fix → Scan and the roll's 12 frames develop one by one, ending on the Developed contact sheet | The engine: films fitted to real camera renders, halation and grain, all rendered on the phone. A before/after slider (As shot ↔ a film) |
| 3 | Prints | Lab counter, daylight | A lab envelope opens and the prints fan out. The same scene is shown through Olive, Lantern, Toffee, Guava, Ink and Pollen, each labelled in grease pencil. A contact sheet follows with circled frames | Film Roll mode: load, shoot blind, develop, the date stamp and borders |
| 4 | Your roll is ready | Lab ticket | A ticket slides out of a printer slot | The action slot, links to Diary, Privacy and Terms, and the language switch |

## 3. Look

- **Tie to the app:** Fonder's amber accent, Bricolage Grotesque for headlines, Be Vietnam Pro for body text, and the app's real canister art.
- **Palette per chapter,** crossfading through a light-leak band:

| Chapter | Colours |
|---|---|
| 0 | Warm charcoal with the amber accent |
| 1 | Charcoal to camera-plastic grey, with LED orange `#FF8A1E` |
| 2 | Safelight red / oxblood, with dim amber |
| 3 | Paper cream, with grease-pencil red and ink |
| 4 | Paper cream and black print, with an amber button |

- **Type:**
  - Headlines in Bricolage Grotesque, large and tight.
  - Body in Be Vietnam Pro.
  - Labels and frame numbers in a monospace face.
  - Date digits are SVG seven-segment shapes, matching the app's date stamp.
  - Grease-pencil notes use a handwritten Google font that must include full Vietnamese diacritics. The candidate is Patrick Hand, to be verified.
- **Textures, all generated:**
  - Film grain on a half-resolution canvas at low opacity.
  - Light leaks from CSS gradients at chapter joins.
  - Paper fibre and a soft vignette on the cream chapters.
  - Sprocket borders drawn in CSS.
- **Photos:**
  - 8–12 renders of Hai's own DSCF RAWs, made with the diary render tool (the app's engine).
  - No identifiable faces; EXIF, GPS and XMP stripped.
  - WebP plus a JPEG fallback, at most 1600 px on the long edge.
  - **Never** use the dpreview samples (the numeric-named RAWs, `fujifilm_x_t5_05`).

## 4. Motion, accessibility, performance

- **Motion:**
  - GSAP ScrollTrigger, scrubbed to scroll: no timers, and scrolling up rewinds.
  - Only transform and opacity animate; there are no layout-changing animations.
  - Grain flickers slowly and pauses when the tab is hidden.
  - Buttons press like camera buttons.
- **Sound:** off. A speaker toggle adds a shutter click and a film-advance sound, and only plays after the visitor taps.
- **Reduce Motion (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`):**
  - No pinning, no scrub and no grain animation.
  - Each chapter renders in its end state (the developed print, the fanned prints) with simple fades.
  - The page reads as a normal scrolling page.
- **No JavaScript:** the page is complete and static, and every chapter shows its end state. JavaScript adds motion only.
- **Accessibility:**
  - All text is HTML, and images have alt text.
  - The before/after slider is a keyboard-operable range input with a label.
  - Contrast is ≥ 4.5:1 for body text on every ground, including the safelight red.
  - Language switch links are real links carrying `lang` and `hreflang`.
- **Performance budget:**
  - The first screen (canister, fonts, grain, CSS, JS) is ≤ 400 KB.
  - The whole page is about 3 MB.
  - Later chapters' images load lazily as they approach.
  - GSAP loads with `defer`.

## 5. The action slot

The site root holds `config.json`:

```json
{
  "stage": "follow",
  "filmCount": 39,
  "instagram": "https://www.instagram.com/fonder.app",
  "threads": "https://www.threads.com/@fonder.app",
  "testflight": "",
  "appstore": ""
}
```

What each stage shows:
- **`follow`:** "TestFlight opens soon — follow Fonder to get the link first", with Instagram and Threads buttons.
- **`testflight`:** a "Join the beta" button linking to `testflight`, with a note that places are limited.
- **`appstore`:** Apple's official "Download on the App Store" badge, used as Apple's guidelines require, linking to `appstore`.

The build refuses a stage whose link is empty. No personal data is collected in any stage, so the privacy policy needs no change.

## 6. English and Vietnamese

- One template, `home/template.html`, with a `{{key}}` for every visible string.
- Strings live in `home/strings.en.json` and `home/strings.vi.json`. The build fails if either file is missing a key.
- `tools/build_home.py`, standard library only, writes:
  - `/index.html` (`lang="en"`)
  - `/vi/index.html` (`lang="vi"`)
  - `hreflang` alternates and `x-default` on both
- **First visit:** if the browser's language starts with `vi` and the visitor is on `/`, a small dismissible bar offers "Xem bằng tiếng Việt". The page never redirects on its own. The choice is kept in `localStorage`, wrapped in try/catch.
- **Terms:**
  - Film names stay in English in both languages.
  - Fonder terms follow the app's `vi-strings.csv`, for example Cuộn phim and tráng phim.
  - Stock film names and camera-simulation names never appear.

## 8. Testing and review before going live

1. Headless Chrome screenshots of every chapter:
   - at 390 px and 1280 px
   - in EN and VI
   - with Reduce Motion on and off
   - with JavaScript on and off
2. A scroll recording (GIF or MP4) of the full page on a phone-sized viewport.
3. Lighthouse on mobile: performance ≥ 90 and accessibility ≥ 90.
4. `build_home.py --check`:
   - all keys present in both languages
   - config valid
   - every image under budget with metadata stripped
   - no stock or simulation names in the strings
5. Hai reviews the screenshots and recording. **Nothing is pushed until he says yes**, because pushing puts it live.

## 9. Out of scope

- Email collection or any backend.
- Analytics or cookies.
- A blog; the diary covers that.
- Per-film pages.
- An App Store press kit.
