The mode dial

A web mock of Fonder's camera, to feel the dial round the shutter. It isn't the app's code: the clicks stand in for the phone's haptics (and a buzz on Android).

Try it

  1. Hold the ring round the shutter (not the shutter) for a quarter of a second. The dial opens.
  2. Turn: slide your thumb sideways, as on the iPhone Camera's zoom dial. A thumb-width a mode, a click for each; the name lands in the viewfinder. Up and down does not turn it.
  3. Let go to select. To cancel, slide back to the dot, or slide well away from the dial first.
  4. A quick tap on the ring shows the dial for a moment; slide on it and it turns at once. A tap on the shutter shoots, in any mode.
  5. Shoot in DOUBLE: after the first frame the dial is held until you keep or discard (the ✕).
